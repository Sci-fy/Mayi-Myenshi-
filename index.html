A life spent keeping Bwatiye culture on its feet

Mayi Myenshi is a content creator and the founder of Huboduwe TV, a platform built to document and celebrate the traditions of the Bwatiye people. Through film, sound, and story, he brings community life to audiences who might otherwise never see it.

Off screen, Mayi is known across Bwatiye communities as one of the finest dancers of their traditional songs. Families and organizers regularly invite him to lead the dance floor at weddings, naming ceremonies, festivals, and community shows — not as a hired stranger, but as someone who understands the meaning behind every step.

Huboduwe TV and Mayi's performances now work side by side: one keeps the culture recorded, the other keeps it alive in the room.
